Psychology · How we judgeSolid
Anchoring
i · ii
In short

The first number you hear pulls your judgment toward it, even when that number is meaningless.

Explained

In a 1974 study, Tversky and Kahneman spun a wheel rigged to stop at 10 or 65, then asked people what share of African countries were in the UN. Those who saw 10 guessed about 25%; those who saw 65 guessed about 45%. The brain starts from whatever number is on the table and adjusts, but not far enough.
