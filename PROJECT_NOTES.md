## 1. Name

**Vorago** (vo-RAH-go): Latin, "abyss, chasm, whirlpool", the deep that swallows things.
- Web address: `letstryitmkn.github.io/Vorago/` (capital V, the address is case-sensitive). Save name `vorago.v1`, offline-cache names `vorago-…`
- Why it fits: the depths beneath the surface where influence works; the chasm to the underworld that ties to the icon (Persephone, pomegranate, lilies)
- Heads-up: Vorago is also a well-known boss in the game RuneScape; gamer friends may recognise it
- Earlier name: **Umbrarum** ("of the shadows", with "Ars Umbrarum" on the loading screen), renamed 2026-09-29. Also considered: Mercurius, Pactum, Animus, Suasoria, Consilium, Umbrae, Penumbra, Suada, Persona, Legatus
## 2. The idea in one line

Bite-sized daily notes on **psychology, persuasion and negotiation**, offline on the phone, shared with friends (different friends from Retia's).

## 3. Decided so far

- **Part of a trio** of learning apps (Retia = 1st, Vorago = 2nd, a 3rd later). Same idea and philosophy, completely different content and design
- **Same learning app as Retia**: daily notes + Recollection, same engine. **Full redesign** (look, and likely its own wording/vocabulary)
- **Pace**: 7 notes a day, 2 pages per note (In short + Explained / Example + Why it matters), new Folio twice a month, same as Retia
- **Daily mix**: 3 psychology / 2 persuasion / 2 negotiation (psychology leads; the other two build on it)
- **Evidence tag** on every note: **Solid / Debated / Legend**
- **"How to install" screen** on first open, for friends (iPhone + Android). Add to Retia later too
- **Separate app from Retia**, not a 4th Retia subject
- **Shared with friends**: just a link + Add to Home Screen; no accounts, each person's progress stays on their own phone
- **Kept apart from Retia on the same web address**: own save name (`vorago.v1`) and own offline-cache names (`vorago-…`), and its clean-up only deletes its own caches. Retia's side fixed in Retia v8

## 4. Open questions

4. **Look**: chiaroscuro / candlelit (late-Renaissance, Caravaggio-style light and dark), the night sibling to Retia's daylight notebook. Proposed, mockups to come
5. **Pace**: two apps = two sets of Folios to write. Same 7 a day and twice a month, or lighter (e.g. 5 a day / monthly)?
6. **Subject split** per day across psychology / persuasion / negotiation
7. **First-open "how to install" screen** for friends (iPhone + Android). Proposed
8. **Start date for friends**: check how Retia counts days so a friend who starts later begins at Day I, not in arrears
